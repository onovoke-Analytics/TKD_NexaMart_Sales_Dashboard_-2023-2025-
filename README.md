TKD NexaMart Sales Performance Dashboard Analysis (2023–2025)

Overview:

This project presents a comprehensive sales and profitability analysis of TKD NexaMart covering the period from 2023 to 2025 using an interactive business intelligence dashboard. The analysis focuses on evaluating customers’ contribution, regional profitability, product category performance, salespersons effectiveness, pricing trends and state-level market performance.
The dashboard was developed to transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights that support strategic planning, operational efficiency, and revenue optimization. Through advanced data visualization techniques, the project provides management with a centralized platform for monitoring business performance across multiple dimensions.
Key dashboard components include customer profitability analysis, regional profit distribution, product category performance, pricing trend evaluation, salespersons rankings and top-performing states. The project demonstrates the practical application of business intelligence tools in identifying growth opportunities, operational risks, and market trends.

Objectives:

The main objectives of this project include:

•	To analyze TKD NexaMart’s sales and profitability performance between 2023 and 2025.

•	To identify the company’s most profitable product categories and customer segments. 

•	To evaluate salespersons productivity and contribution to business performance. 

•	To assess regional and state-level profitability distribution. 

•	To examine pricing trends and their potential impact on profitability. 

•	To identify high-performing and underperforming business areas. 

•	To provide actionable insights that support strategic decision-making and operational improvement.

•	To demonstrate the use of Microsoft Excel and data visualization techniques for business analytics. 

Key Insights:

Customer Contribution Analysis: The analysis revealed that Michael Graham generated the highest customer revenue/profit contribution at $16,982.80, significantly outperforming other top customers. This indicates a concentration of business value within a few high-performing customers.

Salesperson Performance: Among the sales team, Larry Chapman emerged as the top-performing salesperson with approximately $9,990.30 in sales/profit contribution. The results also showed relatively balanced performance among the leading sales representatives.

Product Category Profitability: The Furniture category generated the highest overall profit at $657,551.20, followed closely by Office Supplies and Clothing. Electronics recorded the lowest profitability among the major categories, indicating opportunities for strategic improvement.

Regional Profit Distribution: The South region recorded the strongest profitability contribution at $641,029.10, while the North region generated the lowest profit level. This highlights differences in market performance and operational effectiveness across regions.

Pricing Trend Analysis: The dashboard identified a gradual decline in unit prices from $134.23 to approximately $99.21, suggesting the impact of competitive pricing strategies, discounts or changing market conditions.

State-Level Market Performance: Washington emerged as the highest-performing state with profits exceeding $76,783, followed closely by South Dakota and Colorado. These states represent strong-performing regional markets with significant business potentials.

Outcome:

The project successfully delivered a dynamic and interactive dashboard capable of supporting data-driven business decisions within TKD NexaMart. The analysis provided management with valuable insights into customers behavior, regional performance, pricing trends, product profitability and sales productivity.
The dashboard enables stakeholders to:

•	Monitor sales and profitability performance. 

•	Identify profitable products, customers and regions. 

•	Improve pricing and sales strategies. 

•	Enhance operational efficiency and resource allocation. 

•	Detect underperforming business areas requiring strategic interventions. 

•	Support forecasting, strategic planning and business growth initiatives. 

Overall, the project demonstrates how modern business intelligence tools such as Microsoft Excel can transform complex datasets into meaningful insights that improve organizational performance and strategic decision-making.
